# Fonts

These are fan-made remakes of the fonts used in the D&D 5e books, taken unchanged from
[The Homebrewery](https://github.com/naturalcrit/homebrewery) (`themes/fonts/5e`), which is MIT licensed.

| File | Family used in `css/theme.css` | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Bookinsanity*.woff2 | Bookinsanity | Rules text and numbers |
| Scaly-Sans*.woff2 | ScalySans | Interface, notes, tables |
| Mr-Eaves-Small-Caps.woff2 | MrEavesSmallCaps | Section headings |
| Nodesto-Caps-Condensed*.woff2 | Nodesto | Page titles |

The remakes were made by the D&D fan community (Solbera and others) for free fan use. If you
publish this app somewhere besides your own group, check The Homebrewery's current credits for
the fonts' exact license terms and keep this credit with them.
